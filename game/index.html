NEON BAY HEIST

An original open-world crime game. Rio Kade owes Madame Sol big — steal cars, outrun the Neon Bay police, and pull off four jobs to buy your freedom. Every street, name and story here is invented for this game.

On foot: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, mouse to look (click the game to lock the mouse).
Car: walk up to any car and press E to get in (stealing one in the open raises your wanted level). W/S gas and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, E get out.
Combat: 2 draw pistol, click to shoot, 1 holster, F punch.
Police: stars rise when you commit crimes. Lose them by hiding until they cool off. If a cop grabs you on foot, you're busted.
Missions: pick a job from the list above and follow the yellow beacon. M music, P pause. Progress saves automatically.
Progress saves automatically to this browser.
